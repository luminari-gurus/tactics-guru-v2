## Outcome

Use an owned Healing Draught as a battle action and revive a fallen party member in preparation with an owned Revive Kit. Both work in authored runs and dungeons, including the main menu’s saved journeys. Direct testing routes use the same game rules and keep their progress in memory. An isolated standalone battle has no party inventory. The existing item catalog, preparation controls, and reward flow from #77–#79 are the starting points.

## Proposed rules and non-goals

- An active living hero can drink one Healing Draught from the shared party stack to restore up to 6 of their own HP, capped at their projected maximum. Confirmation spends the hero's action and one item, leaves movement and turn ownership intact, and draws no RNG. Full health, exhausted actions or stock, stale reviews, enemy/presentation locks and terminal battles reject the use without changing HP, ownership, turn, or RNG. Preparation use continues to heal a living party member.
- A Revive Kit works in an existing preparation or reward phase with at least one surviving hero. It targets a fallen party member and restores `max(1, floor(maxHp * 35 / 100))`, capped at the hero's current maximum. The hero keeps their progression and equipment assignment and enters the next battle through the existing survivor projection. A living target, a party wipe, or an active battle cannot use the kit. Show eligibility and the result before confirmation.
- New runs and dungeons start with empty inventories. Make the kit earnable through the existing deterministic two-choice reward flow for newly generated offers, alongside the three current items, using the existing stack limit of nine and one item per claim. Preserve the exact two options of a pending offer saved before this change.
- Keep combat targeting self-only for the draught. Out of scope are throwing or trading items, mid-battle revival, new loot tables for #174, new gear slots, and changes to learned abilities or enemy AI. The item control works with the current #162 picker and both combat control skins.
