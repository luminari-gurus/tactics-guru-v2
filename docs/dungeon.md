Enemy scaling does not modify heroes. Hero HP and Mage caster level follow [hero progression](hero-progression.md); non-HP class stats, movement and resources retain their baselines. Repeated projection always starts from the immutable baseline; it never scales saved damage or a previous projection. This arithmetic contract is not a high-depth balance claim.

Open `/dungeon` (respecting the configured application base path), or follow **Dungeon floors** from `/run`. This is an explicit continuing mode. `/` remains a standalone battle, `/run` remains the terminal three-battle proof of concept, and `/map` remains an independent encounter-free experiment. Their saves are separate.

Each fresh floor saves three selected encounters, including source/roster, map/composition/pool IDs, biome, kind, guardian reference, scaling depth/policy and resolved battlefield hash. Resume validates these facts against their versioned definitions without drawing a new selection. The same projected catalog supplies initial HP, inspection, preview, AI, resolution and replay. Active saves retain damage. New-format games using the legacy opponent policy keep fixed opponents and unscaled enemy stats, including on unvisited floors. Pre-progression saves require recovery/export and their originating build. Rooms exploration geometry, stairs and POIs remain independent of battle selection. Further content expansion remains #170.

Legacy fixed-policy floor plans may defer their derived battlefield with `battlefield: null`: formats 1/2 never stored per-slot battlefield geometry. Normalization retains fixed source/roster/depth facts and resolves the deterministic map only at battle entry, avoiding three full graph validations per historical floor. Active battle geometry and RNG still validate exactly. Existing format-3 legacy plans with full descriptors remain supported and validated; new-policy plans always require their resolved battlefield facts. Save identities, byte/floor limits, read-only inspection and raw-backup preservation remain unchanged.

Cold format-3 reads validate all saved selection/source identities, then fully verify only the current floor's battlefield geometry. Historical hashes remain saved exactly; switching to a historical floor verifies those hashes before travel can be saved or published. A bad historical geometry hash therefore blocks that travel without changing the current floor or durable save. This removes cold-start graph work proportional to history and keeps the admission cache at a bounded 128-entry working set. Descriptor shapes, source/map IDs and layout seeds still reject immediately on every floor.

Walk onto any uncleared `M` encounter marker to start its associated encounter; encounters may be cleared in any order. A cleared marker never relaunches. An explicit Enter encounter control appears only if the player is already on the available encounter cell. The first two victories offer the existing two-choice item reward. Claim once to prepare the next battle. The third victory unlocks descent without a further reward or run victory. A party wipe ends the dungeon. Start new dungeon requires confirmation and replaces only the dungeon namespace. It restores the full starting party and empty inventory. A cancelled choice does nothing.

Ascend is unavailable on floor 1. Travel is unavailable during a battle or pending reward. Descend also requires all three encounters to be cleared. Moving onto an available matching stair automatically ascends or descends in the same accepted movement revision; no separate activation is required. Arrival, rendering and restoring a saved position on stairs never trigger travel. Move off and back onto the stair to travel again; remote stair requests are rejected. Travel retains the departing position and enters the retained position on the destination floor (new floors start upstairs). Revisit restores geometry, position, revealed/visited cells, POIs, completion and stair state, and cannot restart cleared encounters or grant rewards again. Party HP, defeated heroes, inventory and equipment carry through travel. New encounters reset battle-local movement/actions/Guarded state; reload preserves the accepted active battle instead. Equipment is projected once from the immutable base catalog. Healing Draught remains preparation-only and cannot revive a defeated hero.

Exploration uses a single cardinal step per arrow/WASD press while the map is focused, adjacent-cell pointer/touch activation, or a labeled direction button (at least 44×44 CSS pixels). Diagonal, blocked, remote, stale or repeated key input cannot move the player. Keyboard navigation of other controls retains its usual behavior. Battles, pending rewards, defeat and dialogs block movement. Each accepted player step advances living enemies once using a separate deterministic movement RNG; enemies avoid visible stairs. Enemy 1 wanders, enemy 2 chases into the player’s previous cell when that cell is reachable (including when the pursuer is already adjacent to it), and enemy 3 blocks toward the resulting cell and stays when already adjacent. Actual occupancy remains authoritative for both pursuit targets; chase ignores an invalid or out-of-range previous cell and never occupies the player’s current cell. Entering an enemy starts its battle immediately. Waiting, rejected commands, rendering and reload do not advance enemies. Each command binds dungeon ID, floor and revision; a step and its enemy movement or battle entry are saved in one revision.

Each initial/accepted position reveals a Manhattan-radius-two diamond, including through walls; this is awareness, not combat LOS. Revealed/visited cell indices are bounded, sorted and unique. Uncleared encounter cells use `M`, including the guardian; cleared encounters retain `✓` unless an available stair shares the cell, in which case the stair remains visible. Upstairs markers and discovered-place entries appear only on floors 2 and above. Downstairs markers and entries appear only after that floor’s final encounter is cleared. Both stairs still require discovery: unlocking descent does not reveal its cell. These presentation rules are derived again on reload and return travel; travel validation remains unchanged. Known POIs appear in the Discovered places list with location and available/cleared and visited state. Completion derives from the existing encounter boundary. Exploration POIs are seeded without replacement from legal cells excluding both stairs, with stable enemy-1/2/3 IDs and slots. Their exploration-v1 seed is independent of terrain, battle, reward and encounter-selection streams. Reused guardian rosters and existing rewards do not imply new named-boss abilities or vault loot.

Simple/Legacy 8-bit art is view-local. Art changes, delayed/failed image loading and rotation preserve authoritative state and RNG; unavailable artwork uses Simple fallback. `/map` retains its independent generator/biome/art inspection controls. Dungeon discovery never grants scouting, trap or cache rewards (#175).

`dungeon-seeds-v1` hashes the run seed, positive safe-integer depth, purpose and encounter slot. Floor, battle and reward purposes are separate. No mutable battle RNG is consumed by floor generation, rendering, travel or previews. Floor descriptors retain template/generator/biome versions and the map hash. Battle descriptors retain their own source identity/hash. Changes to rules, RNG, catalog or templates invalidate compatibility rather than silently rerolling saved progress.

Reward/equipment boundaries use `<dungeon-id>:floor:<depth>:reward:<slot>:option:<ordinal>`. Commands include dungeon ID, depth/encounter boundary and a monotone safe revision. A repeated claim, completion callback or travel control cannot commit again against a changed boundary. Shared ownership, grant, preparation and equipment-projection primitives retain the existing stack limit of 9 and six equipment instances. A full inventory leaves the reward pending and reports capacity. During preparation or a pending reward, Discard names the owned charm and any equipped hero, focuses Cancel first, and requires explicit confirmation. Confirmation permanently removes that instance and its assignment without granting currency or a replacement, changing HP/RNG, or advancing the pending offer. Claim the chosen reward separately after freeing space. Cancel, stale/unowned requests, active battles and defeat cannot discard items. Confirmed disposal persists through the normal save/recovery path; failed saves remain visibly unsaved. Eligible healing supplies can also free consumable capacity. Reward skipping and rerolling are outside this slice.

Only three resolved floor geometries are retained in the UI cache. All visited-floor descriptors, positions and completion facts remain in the durable snapshot; evicted geometry is regenerated and checked against its saved hash on entry. There is no gameplay depth endpoint. Safe-integer overflow, an encoded 1 MiB UTF-8 envelope limit and actual browser quotas are engineering failures, never victory conditions. Collection validation has a conservative 8,192-floor allocation bound; the byte limit is reached earlier for this descriptor format. No visited-floor history is pruned to fit. Browser storage is finite.

Dungeon envelope v9 binds encounter, scaling, exploration and progression definitions with a canonical checksum. Shaman rules supersede the movement integration's format 8 and the earlier format 7; formats 1–8 are incompatible. Inspection/Continue preserve raw bytes for recovery/export and do not reconstruct XP, reset heroes or write a replacement. Return to the originating build to continue an old save. Current-format legacy exploration remains readable and gains movement state only on an accepted step. Explicit replacement retains the existing confirmation safeguards. Older builds cannot be assumed to read new progress; previous migration evidence remains historical.

Primary and backup keys remain `tactics-guru:phaser:dungeon:{primary,backup}:v1`. The single bounded snapshot includes party/items, every floor's selections/progress, a pending reward and at most one active battle. No floor history is evicted to fit. A movement, spatial encounter-entry or automatic stair-travel candidate is published only after primary write/readback succeeds. Stair entry and travel are saved together; a failed travel save retains the pre-step position and floor. Entry failure never launches a detached battle. Subsequent accepted battle commands retain visible unsaved/recovery semantics if a save fails; resolved commands are not rolled back. Failed or uncertain writes retain the current in-memory floor, block silent retries and offer recovery. If an uncertain write committed, **Continue primary dungeon** adopts that inspected durable state; **Retry saving current progress** explicitly chooses current in-memory state. Backup continuation can lose latest progress and says so. A bounded 128-entry cache stores SHA-256 keys of verified current-floor encounter facts. Historical geometry is verified before that floor becomes current, avoiding the former whole-history scan and sequential-eviction cascade. The cache stores no geometry or progress and cannot grant completion, select opponents or preserve changed facts under an old key; save and floor limits remain unchanged.

Startup is read-only. Invalid/incompatible saves stay exportable and are never automatically removed. Save recovery exports current progress and exact saved bytes, supports compare-before-removal with confirmation, and retains recovery copies after a partial reset/removal. Removing one slot does not remove another mode's data. Quota/security/unavailable errors are visible. Explicit unsaved play permits exploration and battle progress but blocks stair travel until saving is restored. Export before closing an unsaved tab; in-memory recovery copies do not survive navigation. Storage operations are synchronous best effort; a changed primary save blocks a stale tab from silently overwriting progress.
