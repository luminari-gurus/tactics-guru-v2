# Dungeon floors

Issue #159 supplies the encounter foundation in `content/dungeonEncounters.ts` and `domain/dungeonEncounters.ts`, integrated with durable dungeon floors and format-3 saves. The catalog admits existing Forest Ruins (with one dungeon-only access step at 4,2), Woodland Crossing, Ruined Approach and generated Desert rosters, including a goblin guardian. Every pairing passes the existing reachability/survivor/fallback checks. The original standalone and short-run maps remain unchanged. The summary, discovered places and battle heading show the saved biome/depth/kind. Undiscovered POIs remain hidden. Exploration uses existing Forest/Desert treatment; prior games retain Grassland. Missing or failed legacy artwork uses Simple fallback, including Desert's currently unavailable legacy art.

The new policy `dungeon-encounters-v1` seed-selects Forest/Desert first, then alternates by depth. Slots are normal, equally weighted elite/vault, then boss. Versioned biome/kind/map/composition streams use stable sorted IDs and do not consume existing battle/reward/terrain/exploration RNG. Guardian means full-roster victory, with no named-boss abilities or extra loot. `enemy-depth-v1` projects baseline enemy HP/power/accuracy/AC: nearest rounding (half toward positive infinity) of baseline × `(depth+9)/10`, clamped to current bounds; vault then rounds × `6/5` and clamps again. Elite and boss have no extra multiplier. Zero remains zero and signed accuracy saturates at its lower bound. Maximum safe-integer depths saturate before multiplication. Hero stats, attacks, movement and resources are unchanged. Repeated projection always starts from the immutable baseline; it never scales saved damage or a previous projection. This arithmetic contract is not a high-depth balance claim.

Current contract: issues #157, #158 and #159. The continuation plan and QA reports distinguish pure/domain proof from browser route acceptance and identify tested revisions.

Open `/dungeon` (respecting the configured application base path), or follow **Dungeon floors** from `/run`. This is an explicit continuing mode. `/` remains a standalone battle, `/run` remains the terminal three-battle proof of concept, and `/map` remains independent inspection. Their saves are separate.

Each fresh floor saves three selected encounters, including source/roster, map/composition/pool IDs, biome, kind, guardian reference, scaling depth/policy and resolved battlefield hash. Resume validates these facts against their versioned definitions without drawing a new selection. The same projected catalog supplies initial HP, inspection, preview, AI, resolution and replay. Active saves retain damage. Valid older games keep the three fixed opponents and unscaled stats, including on unvisited floors. Rooms exploration geometry, stairs and POIs remain independent of battle selection. Further content expansion remains #170; passive wandering/aggressive pursuit remains #155.

Legacy fixed-policy floor plans may defer their derived battlefield with `battlefield: null`: formats 1/2 never stored per-slot battlefield geometry. Normalization retains fixed source/roster/depth facts and resolves the deterministic map only at battle entry, avoiding three full graph validations per historical floor. Active battle geometry and RNG still validate exactly. Existing format-3 legacy plans with full descriptors remain supported and validated; new-policy plans always require their resolved battlefield facts. Save identities, byte/floor limits, read-only inspection and raw-backup preservation remain unchanged.

Cold format-3 reads validate all saved selection/source identities, then fully verify only the current floor's battlefield geometry. Historical hashes remain saved exactly; switching to a historical floor verifies those hashes before travel can be saved or published. A bad historical geometry hash therefore blocks that travel without changing the current floor or durable save. This removes cold-start graph work proportional to history and keeps the admission cache at a bounded 128-entry working set. Descriptor shapes, source/map IDs and layout seeds still reject immediately on every floor.

Walk onto the numbered encounter markers in order. Later markers are traversable but locked; entering the next available marker starts one encounter. A cleared marker never relaunches. An explicit Enter encounter control appears only if the player is already on the available encounter cell. The first two victories offer the existing two-choice item reward. Claim once to prepare the next battle. Guardian victory unlocks descent without a further reward or run victory. A party wipe ends the dungeon. Start new dungeon requires confirmation and replaces only the dungeon namespace. It restores the full starting party and empty inventory. A cancelled choice does nothing.

Ascend is unavailable on floor 1. Travel is unavailable during a battle or pending reward. Descend also requires a cleared guardian. Walk to the matching stair before activating Ascend/Descend; remote stair requests are rejected. Travel retains the departing position and enters the retained position on the destination floor (new floors start upstairs). Revisit restores geometry, position, revealed/visited cells, POIs, completion and stair state, and cannot restart cleared encounters or grant rewards again. Party HP, defeated heroes, inventory and equipment carry through travel. New encounters reset battle-local movement/actions/Guarded state; reload preserves the accepted active battle instead. Equipment is projected once from the immutable base catalog. Healing Draught remains preparation-only and cannot revive a defeated hero.

Exploration uses a single cardinal step per arrow/WASD press while the map is focused, adjacent-cell pointer/touch activation, or a labeled direction button (at least 44×44 CSS pixels). Diagonal, blocked, remote, stale or repeated key input cannot move the player. Keyboard navigation of other controls retains its usual behavior. Battles, pending rewards, defeat and dialogs block movement. No timer advances exploration while hidden or during navigation. Each command binds dungeon ID, floor and revision; a step into the next encounter produces one revision containing both position and battle entry.

Each initial/accepted position reveals a Manhattan-radius-two diamond, including through walls; this is awareness, not combat LOS. Revealed/visited cell indices are bounded, sorted and unique. Known POIs appear in the Discovered places list with location and available/locked/cleared and visited state. Completion derives from the existing encounter boundary. Exploration POIs are seeded without replacement from legal cells excluding both stairs, with stable enemy-1/2/3 IDs and slots. Their exploration-v1 seed is independent of terrain, battle, reward and encounter-selection streams. Reused guardian rosters and existing rewards do not imply new named-boss abilities or vault loot.

Simple/Legacy 8-bit art is view-local. Art changes, delayed/failed image loading and rotation preserve authoritative state and RNG; unavailable artwork uses Simple fallback. `/map` retains its independent generator/biome/art inspection controls. Dungeon discovery never grants scouting, trap or cache rewards (#175).

`dungeon-seeds-v1` hashes the run seed, positive safe-integer depth, purpose and encounter slot. Floor, battle and reward purposes are separate. No mutable battle RNG is consumed by floor generation, rendering, travel or previews. Floor descriptors retain template/generator/biome versions and the map hash. Battle descriptors retain their own source identity/hash. Changes to rules, RNG, catalog or templates invalidate compatibility rather than silently rerolling saved progress.

Reward/equipment boundaries use `<dungeon-id>:floor:<depth>:reward:<slot>:option:<ordinal>`. Commands include dungeon ID, depth/encounter boundary and a monotone safe revision. A repeated claim, completion callback or travel control cannot commit again against a changed boundary. Shared ownership, grant, preparation and equipment-projection primitives retain the existing stack limit of 9 and six equipment instances. A full inventory leaves the reward pending and reports capacity. During preparation or a pending reward, Discard names the owned charm and any equipped hero, focuses Cancel first, and requires explicit confirmation. Confirmation permanently removes that instance and its assignment without granting currency or a replacement, changing HP/RNG, or advancing the pending offer. Claim the chosen reward separately after freeing space. Cancel, stale/unowned requests, active battles and defeat cannot discard items. Confirmed disposal persists through the normal save/recovery path; failed saves remain visibly unsaved. Eligible healing supplies can also free consumable capacity. Reward skipping and rerolling are outside this slice.

Only three resolved floor geometries are retained in the UI cache. All visited-floor descriptors, positions and completion facts remain in the durable snapshot; evicted geometry is regenerated and checked against its saved hash on entry. There is no gameplay depth endpoint. Safe-integer overflow, an encoded 1 MiB UTF-8 envelope limit and actual browser quotas are engineering failures, never victory conditions. Collection validation has a conservative 8,192-floor allocation bound; the byte limit is reached earlier for this descriptor format. No visited-floor history is pruned to fit. Browser storage is finite.

Dungeon envelope v3 binds encounter catalog, scaling and exploration identity with a canonical checksum. Formats 1/2 first pass their exact original shape/identity and battle validation, then normalize to the fixed/unscaled legacy policy without changing party, ownership, revision, pending rewards or active battle/RNG. Format 1 additionally synthesizes exploration: active/pending encounters acquire their deterministic POI cell and completed POIs become known visits. Continue/inspection never writes. The next accepted transition or explicit retry writes format 3 and preserves the exact prior primary in backup; later writes rotate backup normally, so export old bytes you need to retain. Older builds cannot read format 3: export and retain a matching old-format backup for its matching build, accepting disclosed progress loss. There is no automatic downgrade or promise that new progress can be read by an older build.

Primary and backup keys remain `tactics-guru:phaser:dungeon:{primary,backup}:v1`. The single bounded snapshot includes party/items, every floor's selections/progress, a pending reward and at most one active battle. No floor history is evicted to fit. A movement, spatial encounter-entry or travel candidate is published only after primary write/readback succeeds. Entry failure never launches a detached battle. Subsequent accepted battle commands retain visible unsaved/recovery semantics if a save fails; resolved commands are not rolled back. Failed or uncertain writes retain the current in-memory floor, block silent retries and offer recovery. If an uncertain write committed, **Continue primary dungeon** adopts that inspected durable state; **Retry saving current progress** explicitly chooses current in-memory state. Backup continuation can lose latest progress and says so. A bounded cache stores SHA-256 keys of verified encounter facts, capped by the existing 8,192-floor allocation limit times three encounters. It stores no geometry or progress and cannot grant completion, select opponents or preserve changed facts under an old key. This avoids the former 128-entry cache repeating every graph validation beyond 42 floors; save and floor limits remain unchanged.

Startup is read-only. Invalid/incompatible saves stay exportable and are never automatically removed. Save recovery exports current progress and exact saved bytes, supports compare-before-removal with confirmation, and retains recovery copies after a partial reset/removal. Removing one slot does not remove another mode's data. Quota/security/unavailable errors are visible. Explicit unsaved play permits exploration and battle progress but blocks stair travel until saving is restored. Export before closing an unsaved tab; in-memory recovery copies do not survive navigation. Storage operations are synchronous best effort, not a transaction across tabs or slots.

Implementation boundaries: `domain/dungeon.ts` owns transitions and battle validation, `dungeonSaveEnvelope.ts` owns encoding, `persistence/dungeonStorage.ts` owns effects/readback, and `app/DungeonSession.ts` owns the live battle and accepted callbacks. `BattleSession`/`BattleScene` remain the shared battle engine and presentation. `ui/dungeon.ts` renders exploration/items/stairs and `ui/dungeonMap.ts` owns map input and view-local art; `startDungeonApp.ts` owns route lifecycle/recovery. Device evidence uses Playwright emulation; no physical-device testing is claimed.

Dungeon telemetry collection stays disabled until its event/route taxonomy is selected separately. Existing Privacy & analytics controls remain available for the browser preference used by other modes; this change does not activate collection or introduce new event types.
