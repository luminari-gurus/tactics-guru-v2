# Dungeon floors

Current contract: issue #157, based on main `6bfe127dd3228392ebda6cbee5344afafa84ca48` and this branch's dungeon implementation. The QA report records the tested implementation revision.

Open `/dungeon` (respecting the configured application base path), or follow **Dungeon floors** from `/run`. This is an explicit continuing mode. `/` remains a standalone battle, `/run` remains the terminal three-battle proof of concept, and `/map` remains independent inspection. Their saves are separate.

Each floor currently uses the same versioned three-encounter template: the two generated desert battlefields and authored Ruined Approach. The third encounter is designated the guardian; it does not introduce a new boss roster, depth scaling or biome selection. The floor summary is Rooms/Grassland. Its map, upstairs, downstairs, player position and three encounter completion states are independent of battle geometry. Spatial traversal/fog/contact encounters belong to #158; encounter selection/content expansion belongs to #159/#170.

Complete encounters in order. The first two victories offer the existing two-choice item reward. Claim once to prepare the next battle. Guardian victory unlocks descent without a further reward or run victory. A party wipe ends the dungeon. Start new dungeon requires confirmation and replaces only the dungeon namespace. It restores the full starting party and empty inventory. A cancelled choice does nothing.

Ascend is unavailable on floor 1. Travel is unavailable during a battle or pending reward. Descend also requires a cleared guardian. A stair control moves the departing floor's saved position to that stair and enters the retained position on the destination floor (new floors start upstairs). Revisit restores geometry, completion and stair state, and cannot restart cleared encounters or grant rewards again. Party HP, defeated heroes, inventory and equipment carry through travel. New encounters reset battle-local movement/actions/Guarded state; reload preserves the accepted active battle instead. Equipment is projected once from the immutable base catalog. Healing Draught remains preparation-only and cannot revive a defeated hero.

`dungeon-seeds-v1` hashes the run seed, positive safe-integer depth, purpose and encounter slot. Floor, battle and reward purposes are separate. No mutable battle RNG is consumed by floor generation, rendering, travel or previews. Floor descriptors retain template/generator/biome versions and the map hash. Battle descriptors retain their own source identity/hash. Changes to rules, RNG, catalog or templates invalidate compatibility rather than silently rerolling saved progress.

Reward/equipment boundaries use `<dungeon-id>:floor:<depth>:reward:<slot>:option:<ordinal>`. Commands include dungeon ID, depth/encounter boundary and a monotone safe revision. A repeated claim, completion callback or travel control cannot commit again against a changed boundary. Shared ownership, grant, preparation and equipment-projection primitives retain the existing stack limit of 9 and six equipment instances. A full inventory leaves the reward pending and reports capacity. During preparation or a pending reward, Discard names the owned charm and any equipped hero, focuses Cancel first, and requires explicit confirmation. Confirmation permanently removes that instance and its assignment without granting currency or a replacement, changing HP/RNG, or advancing the pending offer. Claim the chosen reward separately after freeing space. Cancel, stale/unowned requests, active battles and defeat cannot discard items. Confirmed disposal persists through the normal save/recovery path; failed saves remain visibly unsaved. Eligible healing supplies can also free consumable capacity. Reward skipping and rerolling are outside this slice.

Only three resolved floor geometries are retained in the UI cache. All visited-floor descriptors, positions and completion facts remain in the durable snapshot; evicted geometry is regenerated and checked against its saved hash on entry. There is no gameplay depth endpoint. Safe-integer overflow, an encoded 1 MiB UTF-8 envelope limit and actual browser quotas are engineering failures, never victory conditions. Collection validation has a conservative 8,192-floor allocation bound; the byte limit is reached earlier for this descriptor format. No visited-floor history is pruned to fit. Browser storage is finite.

Dungeon envelope v1 uses a canonical checksum and version identity. Primary and backup keys are `tactics-guru:phaser:dungeon:{primary,backup}:v1`. The one snapshot includes party/items, all floor facts, a pending reward and at most one active battle. Primary writes follow backup preservation and exact readback. A travel candidate is published to the screen only after its primary write/readback succeeds. Failed or uncertain writes retain the current in-memory floor, block silent retries and offer explicit recovery. If an uncertain write actually committed, **Continue primary dungeon** can adopt that inspected durable state; **Retry saving current progress** explicitly chooses the current in-memory state instead. Backup continuation can lose the latest progress and says so.

Startup is read-only. Invalid/incompatible saves stay exportable and are never automatically removed. Save recovery exports current progress and exact saved bytes, supports compare-before-removal with confirmation, and retains recovery copies after a partial reset/removal. Removing one slot does not remove another mode's data. Quota/security/unavailable errors are visible. Explicit unsaved play permits battle progress but blocks stair travel until saving is restored. Export before closing an unsaved tab; in-memory recovery copies do not survive navigation. Storage operations are synchronous best effort, not a transaction across tabs or slots.

Implementation boundaries: `domain/dungeon.ts` owns transitions and battle validation, `dungeonSaveEnvelope.ts` owns encoding, `persistence/dungeonStorage.ts` owns effects/readback, and `app/DungeonSession.ts` owns the live battle and accepted callbacks. `BattleSession`/`BattleScene` remain the shared battle engine and presentation. `ui/dungeon.ts` renders summary/items/stairs; `startDungeonApp.ts` owns route lifecycle/recovery. Device evidence uses Playwright emulation; no physical-device testing is claimed.

Dungeon telemetry collection stays disabled until its event/route taxonomy is selected separately. Existing Privacy & analytics controls remain available for the browser preference used by other modes; this change does not activate collection or introduce new event types.
